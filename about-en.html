About BAMI

BAMI Technology was founded in 2008, formerly known as Huayuan Software, focusing on providing one-stop digital intelligent management solutions for enterprise logistics supply chain. With nearly 20 years of precipitation, BAMI has numerous senior logistics supply chain management talents and IT technical experts at home and abroad, and is one of the leading software vendors in China's logistics supply chain digital intelligent management.

With the mission of using technology to reduce logistics costs and make people happy, we have provided logistics supply chain digital intelligent management services for nearly 10,000 enterprises nationwide. BAMI supply chain cloud platform is suitable for manufacturing, wholesale and retail, warehousing and transportation industries.

Jason Li

Founder & CEO

Started programming at 16, with 20 years of software development and 15 years of logistics industry experience. Before 2008, served as IT Director at a well-known domestic logistics company. Founded Guangzhou Huayuan Software Technology Co., Ltd. in 2010, successfully developed Huayuan C9 and CGS systems, serving thousands of enterprises.

In 2015, Huayuan Software was strategically acquired by Alibaba-related logistics technology company. Founded BAMI Technology in 2016, focusing on cross-border supply chain digitalization solutions.
